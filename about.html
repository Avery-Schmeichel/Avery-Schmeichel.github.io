Meet the Artist

Hello, I'm Avery

Avery Schmeichel is a Minnesota based mixed media artist with a focus on collage, creating landscapes, portraits and abstract art with paper, magazine clippings and other found materials. Avery is entering her senior year at Minnesota State University Moorhead pursuing a B.F.A. in Art Education, minoring in Art Therapy and Art History and a certification in Book Illustration. Her work appeared in the Rum River Art Center’s ‘Trash to Treasure’ gallery show (2024), where she was awarded third place in the People's Choice Category. Her work also appeared in the 2026 MSUM Student Juried Show, where she was awarded the President’s Award. Avery has a passion for traveling the world. She was able to study art history in multiple field experiences that took place in France, the Netherlands, Italy and Turkey. Observing the world around her is what drives and inspires her creative work. Everywhere she travels she keeps a journal to write and draw things around her that spark creativity. Currently Avery is finishing up her last year of her B.F.A. at MSUM and plans to be an art teacher after she graduates.
